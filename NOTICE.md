# Notices

- **OpenCelliD data** (`data/opencellid/`): cell-tower records from OpenCelliD (https://opencellid.org),
  licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Derived files in that directory are shared under the same licence.
- **Measured walk-test data** is not part of this public release: the walk trace (positions and times of a
  team member) stays private. The lap-1 calibrated route surfaces and every measured-route result are included,
  but the measured-route runs (`twin.measured_twin`, `twin.measured_v314`) cannot be re-executed from this
  release because they read the raw walk epochs.
